Publications

Here are my published research papers:

Treating the Image as a System: A Hybrid Signal-AI Approach to Mammogram Analysis

In this paper, I propose a hybrid signal-AI approach to mammogram analysis that treats the image as a system. The approach combines traditional signal processing techniques with deep learning methods to improve the accuracy of mammogram analysis. I also provide a detailed analysis of the performance of the proposed approach and compare it with other state-of-the-art methods. Presented in The 2026 International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AAIML 2026), published in conference proceedings and IEEE Xplore.

A new mathematical probability based card counting strategy for blackjack using python

In this paper, we propose a new mathematical probability-based card counting strategy for blackjack using Python. The strategy is based on the concept of probability and uses a simple algorithm to keep track of the cards that have been played. We also provide a detailed analysis of the performance of the strategy and compare it with other popular card counting strategies. Presented in The International Conference on Forthcoming Networks and Sustainability (FoNeS 2022), published in IET Conference Proceedings, available also in IEEEXplore.
